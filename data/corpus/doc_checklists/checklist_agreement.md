# Stage 3 — Agreement and registration preparation — Document Checklist

## Required documents

| Document | Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of the agreement draft | `agreement_draft_ack` | Yes | Signed acknowledgement that the draft has been reviewed. |
| Stamp duty payment receipt | `stamp_duty_receipt` | Yes | Paid online through the state portal; the challan reference is required. |
| Witness identity proof | `witness_kyc` | Yes | Two witnesses, PAN or Aadhaar for each. |
| Bank sanction letter | `bank_sanction_letter` | Yes | Required only for loan-funded bookings. Valid on the registration date. |

## Where to submit

Submit at the registration desk at the project sales office. Stamp duty is paid on the state portal directly.

## How completeness is assessed

A stage is complete when every document above is in `submitted` state and no
submitted document has passed its expiry date. An expired document is treated as
a gap, not as a submission: it must be replaced with a current copy.

## Scope of guidance

This checklist is procedural. It states what is required and where it goes. It
does not interpret any clause of the agreement, advise on stamp duty liability,
or comment on the legal effect of a document. Questions of that kind go to the
legal team.
