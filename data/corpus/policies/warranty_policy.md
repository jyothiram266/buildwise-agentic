# Warranty Policy

**Policy:** POL-WAR · version 1.1.0 · effective 2026-01-01

## Coverage periods

Coverage runs from the date of possession recorded against the booking.

| Component | Coverage from possession | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Structure, RCC frame, load-bearing elements | 60 months | Workmanship and material defects |
| Terrace and wet-area waterproofing | 60 months | Workmanship and material defects |
| Concealed plumbing, CP fittings, sanitaryware | 12 months | Workmanship and material defects |
| Wiring, switches, sockets, DBs | 12 months | Workmanship and material defects |
| Door frames, shutters, window channels | 12 months | Workmanship and material defects |
| Tiling and flooring workmanship | 12 months | Workmanship and material defects |
| Internal paint and putty finish | 6 months | Workmanship and material defects |
| Kitchen modular units, wardrobes, hardware | 12 months | Workmanship and material defects |
| Lift equipment, via the OEM warranty | 24 months | Workmanship and material defects |

Five-year structural defect liability applies from the date of handover under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, section 14(3).

## Exclusions

- Damage from resident alterations, drilling into concealed services, or unauthorised civil work
- Normal wear and tear, and consumables such as bulbs, gaskets and filters
- Damage from force majeure events
- Defects reported after the applicable coverage period has ended

## How a claim is assessed

1. The resident raises a ticket describing the defect.
2. Coverage is computed from the possession date on the booking and the component
   period above. The result is an **indication**, not a decision.
3. A technician inspects and confirms whether the cause falls inside coverage.
4. Confirmation of cover is issued by the customer relations team, in writing.

No automated response confirms warranty cover. The most an automated response
says is that a component appears to fall inside or outside the period, pending
inspection.
