# Aurora Heights — Project Brochure

## Overview

Aurora Heights sits off Whitefield Main Road, within ten minutes of the ITPL corridor and walking distance of two international schools. The three towers are arranged around a central landscaped deck so that no living-room window faces another tower directly.

- **City:** Bengaluru
- **Locality:** Whitefield
- **Type:** apartments
- **Project status:** under construction
